The Clockmaker’s Secret
In a narrow alley where the cobblestones whispered underfoot, there stood a shop that never seemed open yet never closed. Its windows were clouded with dust, and behind them, hundreds of clocks ticked in strange, uneven rhythms. Elias, the old clockmaker, was rarely seen, but those who caught a glimpse swore his eyes held the shimmer of constellations. One evening, a curious traveler pressed her hand against the glass, and the ticking inside fell silent. The door creaked open, revealing a room where time hung in the air like mist, and every clock’s face showed a different moment from her life—some she remembered, others she had yet to live. Elias smiled faintly and said, “Choose wisely. You can keep one moment forever, but it will cost you all the rest.”
